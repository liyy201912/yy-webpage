Yanyu Li

Ph.D., Northeastern University

I am a researcher specializing in efficient deep learning, with a focus on vision transformers, diffusion models, neural architecture search, and model compression for mobile and edge deployment. My work has been published at top venues including NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, AAAI, IJCAI, ECCV, and HPCA.

Research Interests

Efficient Vision Transformers

Designing lightweight and fast vision transformer architectures that achieve MobileNet-level speed with superior accuracy.

Diffusion Models

Accelerating text-to-image and video diffusion models for real-time generation on mobile and edge devices.

On-Device AI

Deploying state-of-the-art deep learning models on mobile phones and edge hardware through compression and optimization.

Model Compression

Neural network pruning, quantization, and neural architecture search for efficient inference.
