Yanyu Li

Ph.D., Northeastern University | Efficient AI for Vision and Generative Models

I am a researcher working on efficient deep learning, with emphasis on vision transformers, diffusion models, neural architecture search, quantization, and hardware-aware deployment. My research aims to translate state-of-the-art machine learning systems to mobile, edge, and resource-constrained platforms while maintaining strong quality and accuracy.

My work includes 59 publications, 2,963 citations, h-index 23, and 7 patents / patent applications, with papers appearing at NeurIPS, CVPR, ICCV, ICLR, AAAI, IJCAI, ECCV, HPCA, FPGA, and FPL.

Research Themes

A scientific focus on efficient model design, generative modeling, and practical deployment for mobile and edge intelligence.

Efficient Vision Transformers

Designing lightweight transformer backbones that achieve mobile-scale latency while preserving strong visual recognition performance.

Diffusion Models & Generative AI

Accelerating and improving text-to-image and video diffusion systems, from mobile deployment to text-conditioned quality control.

Compression, Quantization & NAS

Developing pruning, low-bit quantization, sparse training, and search-based methods for efficient inference and training.

Mobile, Edge & FPGA Deployment

Bridging algorithms and systems through hardware-aware co-design for real-time deployment on phones, edge platforms, and reconfigurable hardware.

Representative Contributions

Introduced mobile-speed transformer architectures through the EfficientFormer line of work, demonstrating that transformer backbones can operate at MobileNet-class latency on real devices.

Advanced efficient diffusion modeling with SnapFusion, TextCraftor, BitsFusion, and related work on mobile text-to-image and video generation.

Built hardware-aware methods across quantization, pruning, sparse training, and FPGA acceleration for practical on-device intelligence.
