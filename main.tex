\section{Research Summary}

Research scientist in efficient AI and generative vision systems with \textbf{60+ publications}, \textbf{2,900+ citations}, \textbf{h-index 23}, and \textbf{7 patents / patent applications}. My work focuses on bringing state-of-the-art deep learning models to mobile and edge platforms through advances in vision transformers, diffusion models, neural architecture search, pruning, quantization, and algorithm-hardware co-design.

% ---------- Research Interests ----------
\section{Research Interests}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Efficient AI:} quantization, pruning, distillation, neural architecture search, and hardware-aware optimization for real-time inference.
    \item \textbf{Vision \& Generative Models:} efficient vision transformers, diffusion models for image and video generation, and quality-efficient training strategies.
    \item \textbf{On-Device Deployment:} translating modern AI systems to mobile, edge, and FPGA platforms with strong latency-quality trade-offs.
\end{itemize}
